# Baja Luxury

Property management for the homes Baja Luxury looks after: what each house costs, and what its homeowner has with us.

## People

**Owner**:
Someone who owns Baja Luxury, the company. Sees and may change everything.
_Avoid_: Admin, boss

**Manager**:
Baja Luxury staff who run the houses day to day.
_Avoid_: Admin, staff member

**Homeowner**:
Someone who owns a house Baja Luxury looks after. Sees only their own houses.
_Avoid_: Owner, client, property owner

## Money

**Balance**:
Our bookkeeping with a homeowner for one house: what they gave us, less what we paid out of it. Below zero, they owe us.
_Avoid_: Account balance, funds

**Paid from the balance**:
An expense Baja Luxury paid with the homeowner's money, so it lowers the balance.
_Avoid_: Balance expense

**Paid directly**:
An expense the homeowner paid themselves, by check or their own card. Recorded so the house's full cost is known; the balance doesn't change.
_Avoid_: Paid apart, off-balance

**Company currency**:
The currency the books are kept in (MXN). Every amount is entered and stored in it.
_Avoid_: Base currency, local currency

**Display currency**:
The currency a person chooses to read amounts in. Converted at the company's one exchange rate, so it is approximate; it never changes what is stored.
_Avoid_: Preferred currency, user currency
